# Privacy policy: Sit Listing Marker for TrustedHousesitters (unofficial)

Last updated: 2 November 2026

**What it stores.** When you mark a listing, the extension saves the listing ID, your choice (interested or not interested), any reasons and note you add, the listing's title, location and URL, and the time you marked it. It also saves your list of reasons.

**Where.** Only in your browser, in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device.

**What it sends.** Nothing. The extension makes no network requests, has no analytics or tracking, uses no remote code, and shares no data with anyone, including TrustedHousesitters and the developer.

**Export.** If you use Export, a JSON file of your marks is saved where you choose. What you do with that file is up to you.

**Deleting your data.** Remove individual marks in the toolbar popup, or uninstall the extension to delete everything it stored.

**Affiliation.** This extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TrustedHousesitters.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/miticv/trusted-house-sitters-extension/issues.
